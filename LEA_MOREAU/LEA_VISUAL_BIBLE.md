## 1. RENDER STYLE

**Photorealistic High-End Lifestyle Photography**

- **Medium:** Professional digital portrait & street photography (full-frame 35mm equivalent sensor).
- **Treatment:** Zero CGI, zero 3D game character or plastic airbrushed look — pure cinematic, authentic photography.
- **Skin:** Glowing, dewy complexion with visible pores, authentic natural micro-texture, gentle blush flush on upper cheeks, soft natural skin realism.
- **Hair:** Glossy dark-chocolate brown strands, natural movement and realistic weight, soft loose French waves, center-parted.
- **Color Grading:** Warm, filmic tone (Kodak Portra 400 / 35mm aesthetic) — soft flattering contrast, natural skin tones.
- **Post-Processing:** Natural editorial grade — no aggressive frequency separation, no plastic smoothing.

## 2. LIGHTING SCHEMAS

### **Schema A: Parisian Daylight / Balcony (Hero Look)**
- **Key Light:** Diffused soft overcast or bright morning daylight from open window or balcony.
- **Fill:** Soft bounce off Haussmann limestone facade and pale walls.
- **Color Temp:** 5200K–5600K (natural daylight).
- **Catchlights:** Crisp natural reflections in pupils.

### **Schema B: Golden Hour Café & Boulevard**
- **Key Light:** Low-angle warm sunlight (3800K–4200K) creating a gentle hair rim light and warm skin glow.
- **Environment:** Outdoor Paris café terrace, street tables, cobblestone pavements with soft background blur.

### **Schema C: Intimate Haussmann Interior**
- **Key Light:** Soft directional window spill paired with warm interior ambient lighting (2700K lamp).
- **Environment:** Parisian apartment, reading nook, morning coffee in kitchen, minimalist linen sofa.
