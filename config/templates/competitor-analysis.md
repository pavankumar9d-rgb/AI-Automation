## MCP Data Sources Used
- `discover_account` — Public profile and recent posts
- `compare_accounts` — Side-by-side metrics for up to 10 accounts
- `get_media` — Recent posts with engagement
- `get_media_insights` — Detailed post metrics
- `search_hashtag` + `hashtag_top_media` — Hashtag performance
- `list_tagged_media` — Competitor mentions/collabs
