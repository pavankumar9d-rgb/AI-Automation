# Reel Hook Templates

## The 3-Second Rule
You have 3 seconds to stop the scroll. Your hook must answer: "Why should I keep watching?"

---

## Hook Categories

### 1. Curiosity Gap
**"Stop doing {{common_mistake}} — here's what actually works"**
- Works for: Educational, tutorials, myth-busting
- Visual: Show the mistake first, then the correction

**"Everyone gets {{topic}} wrong. The real answer might surprise you."**
- Works for: Counterintuitive facts, expert insights

**"I tested {{popular_method}} for 30 days — here's what happened"**
- Works for: Experiments, product reviews, challenges

### 2. Bold Statement/Controversy
**"{{Popular_belief}} is a lie. Here's the truth."**
- Works for: Industry secrets, debunking myths
- ⚠️ Must be factually accurate

**"Unpopular opinion: {{controversial_take}} — and here's why I'm right."**
- Works for: Thought leadership, hot takes

**"Stop wasting money on {{expensive_thing}}. Do this instead."**
- Works for: Budget tips, alternatives, hacks

### 3. Transformation/Before-After
**"From {{starting_point}} to {{result}} in {{timeframe}} — no {{common_excuse}} required."**
- Works for: Fitness, skills, business growth, makeovers

**"Watch me turn {{raw_material}} into {{finished_product}} in 60 seconds."**
- Works for: DIY, cooking, art, coding, crafting

**"Day 1 vs Day {{number}} — the progress nobody sees."**
- Works for: Journey content, consistency proof

### 4. Question/Engagement Bait
**"Quick question: {{specific_question}} — comment your answer."**
- Works for: Polls, opinions, community building

**"POV: You're {{relatable_situation}}. What do you do?"**
- Works for: Skits, relatable humor, decision points

**"Can you spot the {{detail}}? 99% miss it."**
- Works for: Hidden details, Easter eggs, attention tests

### 5. Value Promise/Teaser
**"3 {{things}} that changed how I {{activity}} forever."**
- Works for: Listicles, tips, resources

**"The {{number}}-step framework I used to {{achieve_result}}."**
- Works for: Frameworks, methodologies, systems

**"Save this reel — you'll need it when {{situation}}."**
- Works for: Reference content, checklists, guides

### 6. Story/Emotional Hook
**"I almost quit {{endeavor}} until {{turning_point}} happened."**
- Works for: Personal journey, vulnerability, inspiration

**"Nobody talks about {{hidden_struggle}} — so I will."**
- Works for: Taboo topics, mental health, behind-the-scenes

**"The moment I realized {{epiphany}} changed everything."**
- Works for: Aha moments, pivots, lessons learned

### 7. Authority/Social Proof
**"As a {{credential}}, here's what {{industry}} doesn't tell you."**
- Works for: Expert advice, insider knowledge

**"{{Famous_person/brand}} uses this exact method for {{result}}."**
- Works for: Case studies, celebrity strategies

**"My {{metric}} went from {{before}} to {{after}} using only {{method}}."**
- Works for: Results proof, data-driven content

## Quick Hook Formulas

**Formula A:** [Stop Word] + [Pain Point] + [Promise]
> "Stop scrolling. Struggling with {{problem}}? Here's the fix."

**Formula B:** [Number] + [Timeframe] + [Result] + [No Excuse]
> "3 days. 10K views. Zero ad spend."

**Formula C:** [Visual Demo Start] + [Unexpected Result]
> *Shows messy start* → *Reveals polished result*

**Formula D:** [Relatable Struggle] + [Simple Solution]
> "When {{pain}} but {{easy_fix}}."

**Formula E:** [Secret/Insider] + [Reveal]
> "The {{industry}} secret they don't want you to know."

## Hook Library by Niche

### Tech/AI
- "I built {{app}} in {{time}} with {{tool}} — no code."
- "This AI tool just replaced my {{job_function}}."
- "ChatGPT can't do this. But {{tool}} can."

### Fitness/Health
- "The {{exercise}} everyone does wrong (and how to fix it)."
- "I ate {{calories}} calories for {{days}} days — here's my bloodwork."
- "Stop {{common_habit}}. It's ruining your {{body_part}}."

### Business/Entrepreneurship
- "How I made ${{amount}} in {{timeframe}} with {{model}}."
- "The {{number}} emails that got me {{result}}."
- "Why your {{strategy}} isn't working — and the pivot."

### Creative/Art
- "Watch me turn a ${{cheap_item}} into a ${{expensive_look}}."
- "The {{tool}} trick that saves me {{hours}} hours/week."
- "POV: You're an artist with {{relatable_problem}}."

---

## Anti-Patterns (Don't Do These)
- ❌ "Hey guys, welcome back to my channel" (wastes 3s)
- ❌ "In today's video I'm going to show you" (tells, doesn't show)
- ❌ Generic "Wait for it..." without visual payoff
- ❌ Clickbait that doesn't deliver
- ❌ Starting with your logo/face without context
